Atlas of French AmericaGeohistorical atlases of French speakers in North America

Olivier J. Caron · Department of Geography

Français

From the Great Lakes to the Gulf of Mexico, from New England to Oregon, French-speaking voyageurs, traders, settlers and mill workers left their mark across the continent. This portal brings together geohistorical atlases that map them, each from its own sources.

The atlases form a network: each entry lives in a single atlas, and the others link to it directly instead of copying it. The addresses of the atlases and their entries are stable and can be cited.
